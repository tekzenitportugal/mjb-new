MJB Community

MJB makes donation to Habitat for Humanity

MJB Wood Group has generously donated 14 truckloads of millwork to Habitat for Humanity, contributing an impressive 60,000 lineal feet of primed MDF moulding. "It was a great experience and a completely charitable effort. We’re looking forward to doing much more in the future," said Scott Griggs, President and CEO of MJB Wood Group. This impactful initiative was made possible through the participation of MJB’s facilities in Clio, SC, El Dorado, AR, and Cedar Hill, TX, showcasing the company’s commitment to giving back to the community.

MJB Community

MJB volunteers with the Home Depot Foundation

MJB proudly partners with The Home Depot Foundation, dedicating time and resources to support their mission of "ensuring safe, affordable housing for our nation’s homeless and combat-wounded veterans." Our team participates in constructing homes, enhancing community spaces, and beautifying properties to create welcoming and functional environments for those who have served. By working alongside this impactful organization, we help provide critical resources and support to veterans in need, fostering a sense of stability and dignity. At MJB, we believe in giving back to the community and honoring the sacrifices made by our nation’s heroes through meaningful action and collaboration.

To learn more about The Home Depot Foundation, visit https://lnkd.in/gfPN3Y87

MJB Community

MJB employees volunteering at a Hearts and Hammers House

MJB employees are deeply committed to making a positive impact in the community, bringing their skills and passion to causes that matter most. As a company that proudly supplies the building industry with high-quality wood materials, we understand the transformative power of a strong, well-built home. Leveraging our expertise, we actively participate in initiatives like home construction and property improvement projects, ensuring that those in need have safe and reliable places to live. At MJB, giving back is not just a responsibility—it’s a reflection of our values and dedication to using our industry knowledge to help build better lives for others.

MJB Community

Building hope together: supporting Family for Families

Every year, MJB facilities across the country come together to support Family for Families, an organization dedicated to providing aid and resources to children and families in need. Through fundraising events, donation drives, and volunteer efforts, each facility actively participates in making a difference in their local communities. From organizing charity auctions to hosting team challenges, our employees showcase their creativity and generosity to raise significant funds for this meaningful cause. At MJB, we believe in the power of collective action and are proud to unite as a company to help Family for Families create a lasting impact on the lives of those they serve.
