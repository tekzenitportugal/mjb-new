Fixture & furniture

MJB’s Fixture & Furniture Business Unit specializes in providing manufacturers with high-quality, customizable wood components for a wide range of applications.

From retail fixtures to healthcare, education, and industrial furniture, our products meet the demands of each industry with durability, precision, and sustainability.
