What sets
us apart

Unmatched expertise

With more than five decades in the wood products industry, MJB Wood Group has cultivated unmatched expertise and a deep understanding of the evolving demands across various markets. Our extensive experience allows us to deliver precision-engineered components crafted with exceptional attention to detail and designed to meet exacting standards.

Whether serving global manufacturers or local businesses, we excel at developing innovative, customized solutions that address the unique challenges of each customer. From optimizing production efficiency to ensuring superior product quality, our team is dedicated to providing reliable, forward-thinking solutions that drive success and foster long-term partnerships.

American based, globally sourced

Operating across the United States and Mexico, and sourcing from over 20 countries worldwide, MJB Wood Group combines the strength of a global network with the dedication of a local partner. Our extensive reach allows us to access high-quality materials from trusted suppliers around the world, ensuring our customers benefit from the best products available in the market.

At the same time, we understand the value of building and maintaining personal relationships with our partners, offering tailored solutions and responsive support. This unique combination of global perspective and local focus enables us to adapt to diverse customer needs, address challenges efficiently, and deliver consistent value, no matter where our partners are located or how complex their requirements may be.

Sustainability at the core

We’re dedicated to responsible sourcing and manufacturing, ensuring our practices protect the environment and support sustainability. By offering FSC®-certified products, we guarantee that our wood materials come from responsibly managed forests, promoting biodiversity and ecosystem preservation.

Additionally, our manufacturing processes incorporate eco-friendly materials, minimize waste, and reduce emissions, aligning with both industry standards and customer environmental goals. With these initiatives, we help our partners achieve sustainability milestones while delivering the high-quality, innovative wood solutions they need.

Customized solutions

We provide a wide range of products, from raw materials to finished components, tailored to meet the exact specifications of our diverse customers. With options like custom sizing, finishes, and precision machining, our solutions are designed to optimize manufacturing processes and deliver superior quality.

Complementing our products, we offer value-added services such as Just-In-Time Delivery and Managed Inventory, ensuring efficient operations and reduced costs for our partners. Together, our innovative products and reliable services empower manufacturers to streamline production, enhance efficiency, and achieve their project goals with confidence.
