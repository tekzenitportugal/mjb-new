Millwork & doors

MJB’s Millwork Business Unit offers a comprehensive range of millwork products, including door components and profiled mouldings.

Our production lineup features primed mouldings, boards, jambs, door cores, lock blocks, and stiles, providing the essential components for interior and exterior door manufacturing. We offer a complete solution for your millwork needs.
