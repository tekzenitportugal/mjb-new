Medium Density Fiberboard (MDF)

MDF is a reconstituted wood panel product made by refining hardwood and softwood fibers, mixing them with wax and resin, and pressing them under heat and pressure to form dense, uniform panels.

MADE FROM

Hardwood and softwood fibers
Wax and resin

KEY QUALITIES

Easy to machine, with smooth edges and a uniform surface

USED FOR

Cabinetry, moldings
