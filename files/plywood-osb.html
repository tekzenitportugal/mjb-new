Oriented Strand Board (OSB)

Oriented Strand Board (OSB) is an engineered wood product manufactured by compressing wood flakes with adhesives in a specific, layered orientation.

MADE FROM

Wood flakes
Adhesives

KEY QUALITIES

Stronger and more durable than particleboard. Consistent, cost-effective and versatile

USED FOR

Flooring, wall sheathing, sidewalls

Plywood

Plywood is an engineered wood product created by layering thin sheets of wood veneer, or “plies,” with each layer’s grain at right angles to the next.

MADE FROM

Thin wood veneers (plies) in cross-grain layers

KEY QUALITIES

Strong and stable, resists warping.
Durable, versatile and cost-effective

USED FOR

Flooring, roofing, wall sheathing, furniture
