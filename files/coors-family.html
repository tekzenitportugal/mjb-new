Since joining the Coors family of companies, MJB has been guided by more than 150 years of family values, innovation and long-term thinking.

A legacy of innovation

Humble Beginnings: The Birth of Coors Brewery

Adolph Coors, a penniless brewer’s apprentice, stowed away on a ship from Germany in 1868 and made his way west, coming upon the perfect water of Clear Creek in Golden, Colorado, at the foot of the Rocky Mountains. He opened Coors brewery in 1873.

A legacy of innovation

CoorsTek: The Beginning

In 1910, the Coors Family made its initial investment in CoorsTek, a highly innovative manufacturer of technical ceramics. By leveraging expertise in engineering and materials science, CoorsTek provides solutions to complex technical challenges in the semiconductor, medical, automotive, aerospace, and other industries.

A legacy of innovation

Resilience Through Dry Times

Prohibition hits Colorado in 1916 and then the nation in 1919. Coors survived by focusing on CoorsTek and producing malted milk which was sold to soda fountain and candy companies until prohibition was lifted in 1933.

A legacy of innovation

Patriotism and Progress: Coors’ Legacy of Support and Sustainability

In 1941, Coors aided the war effort by setting aside half of all beer brewed for the military. Now that’s supporting our troops!

As you take a sip from your next can of beer, you can thank Bill Coors for its fresh taste. In 1959, the two-piece recyclable aluminum can designed by Coors made its debut with a 7-ounce Coors Banquet, a move that revolutionized the beverage container industry. Because of his passion for the environment, Bill Coors wanted to make sure other beverage makers would join Coors’ efforts to start a recycling revolution. His big bet paid off.

A legacy of innovation

Ceramic Armor: Protecting Those Who Serve

In 1966, CoorsTek pioneered a breakthrough in lightweight protection with the development of ceramic armor components for military applications. These innovative materials provided enhanced protection for service members while reducing the weight of traditional armor systems. Building on this legacy, CoorsTek continues to play a critical role in defense by supplying the U.S. military with advanced ceramic armor solutions. These components are engineered to neutralize a range of ballistic threats, including armor-piercing rounds and IED shrapnel, ensuring the safety and effectiveness of those who serve.
