Our lumber
product line

Softwood

Softwood is strong, versatile and widely known for its adaptability. Grown globally,
it suits both interior and exterior uses.

MADE FROM

Pine, cedar, redwood and Douglas fir

KEY QUALITIES

Easy to work with, lightweight and cost-effective.
Fast-growing and widely available

USED FOR

Furniture, flooring, decking, framing, mouldings

Hardwood

Hardwood is one of the most adaptable materials, available in a variety of species, attributes and colors for nearly endless design possibilities.

MADE FROM

Meranti, oak, maple, poplar, walnut and birch

KEY QUALITIES

Dense, strong and durable, with distinctive grain patterns and color consistency

USED FOR

Veneers, furniture, flooring, construction, RVs
