Structural panels

MJB’s Structural Panels delivers high-performance engineered wood products tailored to the needs of manufacturers across diverse industries.

Backed by a North American supplier network, we ensure a steady supply of oriented strand board and plywood, minimizing disruptions and meeting project deadlines.

Key applications

Everything your line needs, in one place.

Furniture frames

Strong, lightweight panels for durable, stable furniture frames, customizable for residential and commercial applications.

Structural panels for vehicles

MJB delivers strong and reliable structural panels for vehicle manufacturing. Designed for floors, walls, and other structural components in recreational vehicles, trailers, and utility vehicles, our durable and precise panels can be customized to meet specific requirements, offering a dependable and cost-effective solution.

Modular housing

MJB supplies high-quality oriented strand board structural panels designed for the modular housing industry. Offering exceptional strength and durability, our panels are ideal for walls, flooring, and roof systems in prefabricated homes. Customizable in size and specifications, these products ensure efficient construction and long-lasting performance.

Commodity oriented strand board

MJB offers commodity oriented strand board panels that deliver reliable performance across a range of general-purpose applications. Perfect for wall sheathing, roof decking, and structural support, our panels meet industry standards while providing cost-effective solutions for construction and manufacturing.

Flooring

MJB offers tough and durable oriented strand board flooring panels engineered for strength and stability. Designed to meet the demands of residential, commercial, and industrial applications, our panels ensure consistent quality, easy installation, and long-lasting performance.
